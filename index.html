A NOTE FROM ADELE

Made with care.
Meant to be shared.

It started with a little wax, a kitchen table, and the wish to make something that felt like home. Now every candle is still poured by hand, in small batches, with a lot of care in every detail.

I make each piece to bring warmth to ordinary days and a little wonder to the special ones. Thank you for letting my work find a place in your home.
